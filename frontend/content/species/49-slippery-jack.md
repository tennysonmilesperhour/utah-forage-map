## Identification

The chestnut to chocolate-brown cap is covered with thick slime in wet weather. The pores are small and yellow. A large, membranous ring hangs from the stem, white above and purplish brown below, and the stem above the ring has small glandular dots.

## Uses and ecology

Slippery jacks are mycorrhizal with pines and traveled with pine plantations to South America, southern Africa, Australia, and New Zealand, where they are now common. Harvest young, firm specimens.

## Memorable fact

In Chile and South Africa, slippery jacks from pine plantations are commercially collected and exported.
