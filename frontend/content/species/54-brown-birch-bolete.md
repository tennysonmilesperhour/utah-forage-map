## Identification

The cap is dull brown, dry to slightly greasy. The long white stem is covered with gray to black scabers. Pores are whitish, aging gray-brown, and the flesh barely changes color when cut. It grows with birch.

## Uses and ecology

Brown birch boletes are among the most common boletes wherever birch grows. Young caps are best; older ones become waterlogged and soft.

## Memorable fact

Because it follows birch, it turns up in city parks and gardens far from forest.
