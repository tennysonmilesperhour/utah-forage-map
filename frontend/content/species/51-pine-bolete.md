## Identification

The cap is dark wine red to mahogany with a slightly wrinkled, often frosted surface. The stem is stout and swollen, covered with a fine white to reddish net. Pores are white when young, turning yellowish olive, and do not bruise blue. The flesh is white and firm.

## Uses and ecology

Pine boletes are part of the porcini group and are cooked and dried like king boletes. They form partnerships with pines and spruces, often on sandy or rocky soil.

## Memorable fact

Porcini markets in Europe mix several species, including pine, summer, and king boletes.
