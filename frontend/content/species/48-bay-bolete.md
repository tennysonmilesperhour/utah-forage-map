## Identification

The cap is chestnut to bay brown, dry and velvety, becoming sticky when wet. Pores are cream to lemon yellow and bruise blue-green when pressed. The stem is streaked brown without a net. The cut flesh may turn faintly blue.

## Uses and ecology

Bay boletes form partnerships with conifers across Europe and in parts of North America. They are commonly eaten in Europe and dry well. Like other mushrooms, they can accumulate radioactive cesium in affected regions.

## Memorable fact

After the Chernobyl accident, bay boletes were monitored because they accumulate cesium more than many other mushrooms.
