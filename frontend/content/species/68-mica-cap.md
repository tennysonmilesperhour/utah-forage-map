## Identification

The caps are tawny to honey brown, bell-shaped, and finely grooved. Fresh caps glitter with tiny white granules like mica, which wash off in rain. Gills turn black and slowly dissolve. They grow in dense clusters from wood.

## Uses and ecology

Mica caps are one of the most common urban mushrooms, appearing on stumps and lawns over buried roots. They must be cooked soon after picking.

## Memorable fact

The glittering granules are fragments of the veil that covered the young cap.
