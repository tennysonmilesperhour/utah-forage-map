## Identification

The cap is bright scarlet, smooth, and waxy. The gills are thick, waxy, and widely spaced, orange-red with yellow edges. The stem is red, fading toward the base. It grows in short grass.

## Uses and ecology

Waxcaps indicate grasslands that have not been plowed or fertilized for decades. Many European countries survey waxcaps to find grassland worth protecting.

## Memorable fact

A lawn with several waxcap species is often older than the house beside it.
