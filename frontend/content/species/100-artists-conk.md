## Identification

The bracket is flat to hoof-shaped and can be enormous. The top is dull gray-brown and crusty, often dusted brown with its own spores. The underside is a bright white pore surface that turns dark brown instantly when scratched. New pore layers grow each year.

## Uses and ecology

Artist's conk causes white rot and can live for many years on one tree. Artists scratch drawings into the fresh white pore surface, which dry permanently.

## Memorable fact

A single conk can release trillions of spores in a season, dusting the top of the bracket and nearby leaves brown.
