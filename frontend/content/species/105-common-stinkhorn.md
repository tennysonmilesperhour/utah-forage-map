## Identification

It starts as a white, soft egg half buried in soil. Cut open, the egg shows a jelly layer around a developing stalk. The stalk bursts out in hours, white and spongy, topped by a cap covered in dark olive, foul-smelling slime.

## Uses and ecology

Flies spread stinkhorn spores after feeding on the slime. The smell can be detected many meters away. Stinkhorns decompose buried wood.

## Memorable fact

The stalk can emerge from the egg in a few hours.
