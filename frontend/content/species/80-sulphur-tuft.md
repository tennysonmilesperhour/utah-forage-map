## Identification

Caps are sulphur yellow with an orange to tawny center. Young gills are sulphur yellow to greenish, darkening to purple-brown as spores mature. It grows in dense tufts on stumps and wood. The taste is very bitter.

## Why it matters

Sulphur tuft is one of the most common wood mushrooms in Europe and North America. It causes gastrointestinal poisoning, and serious cases have been reported.

## Memorable fact

It can suppress competing wood-decay fungi, and researchers study it as a natural control for tree pathogens.
