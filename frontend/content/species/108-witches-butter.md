## Identification

It forms a bright yellow to orange, wavy, brain-like mass of gelatinous lobes on dead hardwood branches. In dry weather it shrinks into a hard orange crust, then swells again after rain.

## Uses and ecology

Witches' butter is a parasite of crust fungi (Peniophora) that grow on dead wood. It is edible but has little flavor and is mostly used for texture.

## Memorable fact

It does not decay wood itself; it feeds on the crust fungi that do.
