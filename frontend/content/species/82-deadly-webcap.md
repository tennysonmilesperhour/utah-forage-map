## Identification

The cap is orange-brown to tawny with a pointed central bump. Gills are rusty brown. The stem is yellowish with faint bands of orange veil. Young specimens have a cobweb-like veil between cap and stem. Spores are rusty brown.

## Why it matters

Orellanine attacks the kidneys. Symptoms can take two days to three weeks to appear, by which time damage may be permanent. People have needed kidney transplants after mistaking this mushroom for chanterelles.

## Memorable fact

The delay between meal and symptoms is so long that the link to a mushroom is often missed at first.
