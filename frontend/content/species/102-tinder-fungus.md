## Identification

The bracket is shaped like a horse's hoof, gray to brown with concentric ridges marking years of growth. The underside is a brown pore surface. Inside, a soft felty layer sits under the hard crust.

## Uses and ecology

Tinder fungus decays birch and beech. Its inner layer, called amadou, was processed into a felt used to catch sparks and carry fire, and for hats and wound dressings.

## Memorable fact

Ötzi the Iceman carried tinder fungus as part of a fire-starting kit.
