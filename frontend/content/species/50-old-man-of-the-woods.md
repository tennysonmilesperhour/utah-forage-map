## Identification

Coarse, woolly gray to black scales cover the cap, and a shaggy veil hangs from its edge. The pores are gray-white and bruise red, then black. The stem is shaggy below a ring zone. Cut flesh turns reddish and then black.

## Uses and ecology

This distinctive bolete is mycorrhizal with oaks in eastern North America, Europe, and Asia. It is edible but blackens when cooked and is not highly rated.

## Memorable fact

Its black spore print is unusual among boletes, most of which drop olive to brown spores.
