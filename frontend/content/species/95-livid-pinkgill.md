## Identification

The cap is large, pale gray to buff, with a silky surface. Young gills are yellowish, turning salmon pink as spores mature. The smell is mealy. The spore print is pink, the decisive check.

## Why it matters

This species is responsible for a large share of mushroom poisonings in parts of Europe. It causes severe gastrointestinal illness that can last days.

## Memorable fact

Its pleasant, mealy smell resembles several edibles, which adds to its danger.
