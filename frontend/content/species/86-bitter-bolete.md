## Identification

The cap is tan to brown. Pores start white and become pinkish with age. The stem carries a coarse dark brown net. Touch a tiny piece to your tongue and spit it out: it is intensely bitter.

## Why it matters

Bitter bolete is the most common reason porcini collections get ruined. It is mycorrhizal with conifers and hardwoods across Europe and eastern North America.

## Memorable fact

The species name felleus means bile-like, after its bitterness.
