## Identification

Everything is white. The cap is conical to bell-shaped, often lopsided. The stem is shaggy with woolly scales and carries a fragile ring that may disappear. The base sits in a large, loose, sac-like volva, often buried. Gills are white and free.

## Why it matters

Like the death cap, this species contains amatoxins that destroy liver cells. Symptoms begin 6 to 24 hours after eating, followed by a deceptive improvement and then liver and kidney failure.

## Memorable fact

Its old English name, destroying angel, was given for its combination of purity of color and extreme toxicity.
