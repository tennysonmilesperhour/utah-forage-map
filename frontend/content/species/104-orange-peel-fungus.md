## Identification

The cups are bright orange inside and pale, finely downy outside, becoming wavy and split with age. They grow in groups on bare soil, often along paths and gravel.

## Uses and ecology

Orange peel fungus is a soil decomposer that colonizes disturbed ground. It is edible but thin; many foragers leave it for its color.

## Memorable fact

Its bright orange comes from carotenoid pigments, the same family that colors carrots.
