## Identification

The cap is pale lemon yellow to white, often with flat patches of veil. Gills are white. The ring is skirt-like. The stem ends in a large, rounded bulb with a sharp rim where it meets the stem. Crushed flesh smells of raw potato.

## Why it matters

False death cap is not considered seriously toxic, but it teaches the most important Amanita lesson: always examine the stem base. It is one of the most common Amanita in European woods.

## Memorable fact

The white form, var. alba, is even easier to confuse with the destroying angels.
