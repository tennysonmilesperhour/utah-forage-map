## Identification

The cap is tawny orange to reddish brown, dry and finely scaly. Gills are rusty orange, becoming darker as spores mature. The stem is yellowish with little veil. Spores are rusty brown.

## Why it matters

Fool's webcap was the first mushroom in which orellanine was identified, after a poisoning outbreak in Poland in the 1950s. Kidney damage can be permanent.

## Memorable fact

Orellanine was named after this species.
