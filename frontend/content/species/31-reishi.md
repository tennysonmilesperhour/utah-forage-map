## Identification

Look for a kidney- or fan-shaped shelf with a shining, lacquered surface in red, mahogany, or orange, often with a white to yellow growing edge. Many shelves have a short lateral stalk that is also varnished. The underside is a fine white pore surface that bruises brown when scratched. Host wood matters: this species favors hemlock and other conifers.

## Uses and ecology

Reishi is too tough to eat and is traditionally simmered for tea or extracted. It decays dead conifer wood and returns in the same log for several years. Clinical evidence for most health claims remains limited, and supplement products vary widely in species and quality.

## Memorable fact

The glossy surface comes from a resin-like crust that forms as the shelf matures, which is why fresh specimens can look almost wet.
