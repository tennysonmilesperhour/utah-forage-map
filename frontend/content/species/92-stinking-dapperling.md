## Identification

The cap is small, rarely over 5 cm, white with a smooth brown center and concentric rings of small brown scales. Gills are white and free. The thin stem has a fragile ring that is easily lost. The smell is unpleasant, like rubber or gas.

## Why it matters

Stinking dapperling itself causes gastrointestinal poisoning, and it belongs to a group of small Lepiota that includes species with amatoxins. Rule: never eat a small parasol.

## Memorable fact

Its smell is often the first clue that a small parasol-like mushroom is a Lepiota.
