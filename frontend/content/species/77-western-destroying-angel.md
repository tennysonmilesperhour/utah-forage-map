## Identification

The cap is white, sometimes tan to yellowish in the center, smooth, and rounded. Gills are white and free. A thin ring may be present or absent. The stem base sits in a thick, white, sac-like volva, often buried in soil.

## Why it matters

This species has caused fatal poisonings in California. It grows in the rainy season when many mushroom hunters are active under oaks. Amatoxin symptoms are delayed for hours.

## Memorable fact

Its spring fruiting makes it one of the few deadly Amanita active outside the autumn season.
