## Identification

Young caps are wine-red to burgundy, fading to tan. Gills start grayish lilac and darken to purple-black. The thick white stem carries a ring whose upper surface is split into ridges like a cogwheel, and white cords spread at the base. Spores are dark purple-brown.

## Uses and ecology

Wine caps are a popular garden mushroom, grown in wood-chip beds, and they help build soil. Wild patches appear in landscaping mulch. Pick young caps.

## Memorable fact

Gardeners grow wine caps between vegetable rows, where the mycelium breaks down wood chips into compost.
