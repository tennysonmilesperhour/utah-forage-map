## Identification

The cap is variable, from wine red and purple to olive brown. Gills are cream to yellowish and brittle. The flesh and stem bruise yellow, then brown, and the stem is often flushed pink. Older specimens smell distinctly of shrimp or crab.

## Uses and ecology

Shrimp russulas are mycorrhizal with conifers and are well regarded among edible Russulas. The fishy smell fades with cooking.

## Memorable fact

The seafood smell gets stronger as the mushroom ages.
