## Identification

The cap is brown and often sticky, with a margin that stays rolled inward. The gills run down the stem, are yellowish to olive, bruise brown, and can be peeled from the cap in one sheet. The stem is short.

## Why it matters

Brown roll-rim was eaten widely in eastern Europe. Raw specimens cause gastrointestinal poisoning, and after repeated meals some people develop immune hemolysis and kidney failure, which has been fatal.

## Memorable fact

The German mycologist Julius Schäffer died in 1944 after eating this mushroom.
