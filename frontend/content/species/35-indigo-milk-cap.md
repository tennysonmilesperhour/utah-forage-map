## Identification

Everything about the fresh mushroom is blue: cap, gills, stem, and flesh. The cap has concentric darker zones and becomes funnel-shaped with age. Cut the gills and a blue latex appears; wounds slowly turn green. Older, sun-faded caps look silvery gray, so the latex is the key check.

## Uses and ecology

Indigo milk caps form mycorrhizal partnerships with oaks and pines. They are sold in markets in Mexico and Guatemala. The flesh is somewhat grainy and holds its color best when sliced thin and cooked quickly.

## Memorable fact

The blue pigment is an azulene compound, one of the few natural blue pigments found in mushrooms.
