## Identification

The cap is white to cream, slowly yellowing when handled, and smells of aniseed or almond. Gills are free, pale grayish pink when young and chocolate brown with age, never pure white at maturity. The large ring has a cogwheel pattern underneath. There is no volva at the base.

## Uses and ecology

Horse mushrooms are grassland decomposers, often in rings. They are a traditional edible in Europe. Like other Agaricus, they can concentrate cadmium from polluted soils, so avoid roadsides.

## Memorable fact

Its yellow staining is slow and gentle, unlike the instant chrome yellow of the yellow stainer.
