## Identification

The fruiting body branches repeatedly in a candelabra pattern. Each branch tip ends in a small cup with points, like a crown. It is creamy to pale tan and grows directly from rotting wood.

## Uses and ecology

Crown-tipped coral decays hardwood, especially aspen. It tastes peppery raw, which disappears with cooking.

## Memorable fact

It is the only common coral fungus with crown-shaped tips, which makes it one of the easiest corals to recognize.
