## Identification

The parasol starts as a brown drumstick and opens into a broad, flat cap with a dark central bump and concentric brown scales on a pale background. The long stem is banded with a brown snakeskin pattern. The thick double ring can be slid up and down the stem. Gills are white and free from the stem.

## Uses and ecology

Parasols recycle organic matter in grassland and open woods across Europe and parts of Asia and North Africa. Caps are often fried whole; stems are fibrous and usually discarded.

## Memorable fact

Few gilled mushrooms in Europe grow taller; a mature parasol can stand 40 cm high.
