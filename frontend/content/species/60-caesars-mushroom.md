## Identification

The cap is smooth, bright orange to red, usually without warts. Gills and stem are golden yellow, and a large white, sac-like volva encloses the base. Young specimens emerge from a white egg; cut vertically, the inside shows orange and yellow.

## Uses and ecology

Caesar's mushroom is mycorrhizal with oaks and chestnut in warm regions. It is protected or regulated in some countries. Its egg stage is especially valued, which is when confusion with deadly Amanita eggs is most dangerous.

## Memorable fact

Roman emperors reportedly reserved this mushroom for themselves, giving it the name Caesar's.
