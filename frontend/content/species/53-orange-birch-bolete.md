## Identification

The cap is orange to orange-brown with an overhanging margin. The tall white stem is covered in black scabers, small rough flecks. The cut flesh turns pinkish, then gray to black. It grows only with birch.

## Uses and ecology

Orange birch boletes are eaten widely in northern and eastern Europe after thorough cooking. The flesh turns dark in the pan. Illness has been reported after undercooked meals.

## Memorable fact

Its flesh turns almost black when cooked, which surprises cooks expecting a bright orange dish.
