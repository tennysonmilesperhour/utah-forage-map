## Identification

The cap is a broad cream fan covered with concentric, feather-like brown scales, often growing in overlapping tiers. The short stem is off-center and blackish at the base. Underneath, the cream pores are large and angular, easily seen with the naked eye. The fresh flesh smells like watermelon rind or cucumber.

## Uses and ecology

Dryad's saddle causes white rot in hardwoods and often appears in the same season as morels. Harvest the soft outer edge of young caps and slice thinly. It is widely distributed across the Northern Hemisphere.

## Memorable fact

The cucumber-like smell is strong enough that some foragers find the mushroom by scent before sight.
