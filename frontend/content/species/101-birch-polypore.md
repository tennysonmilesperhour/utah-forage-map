## Identification

The bracket is kidney-shaped to rounded, smooth, and white to pale tan, with a margin that rolls under the white pore surface. It grows only on birch, often high on dead trunks.

## Uses and ecology

Birch polypore causes brown rot in dead birch and speeds their decay. Its tough surface was once used to strop razors, and it has been used as tinder and in traditional remedies.

## Memorable fact

Ötzi, the 5,300-year-old Iceman found in the Alps, carried pieces of birch polypore threaded on a leather strip.
