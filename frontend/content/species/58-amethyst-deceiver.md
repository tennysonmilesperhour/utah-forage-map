## Identification

When fresh and moist, every part is a deep amethyst violet. The gills are thick and widely spaced, and the stem is tough and fibrous. As it dries, the cap fades to pale lilac or buff, which is how it deceives. Spores are white.

## Uses and ecology

Amethyst deceivers are mycorrhizal with many trees. They are small but colorful in salads when cooked. Because some Laccaria accumulate arsenic, avoid collecting from mining or contaminated sites.

## Memorable fact

The name deceiver comes from how much its color changes as the cap dries out.
