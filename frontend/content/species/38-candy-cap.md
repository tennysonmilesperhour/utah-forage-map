## Identification

Candy caps are small, with dry orange-brown caps that often have a slight central bump. Break a gill and a clear, watery latex seeps out rather than white milk. The stem is hollow and brittle. When dried, the mushroom smells strongly of maple syrup or fenugreek.

## Uses and ecology

Candy caps are mycorrhizal and saprobic along the Pacific coast and are used almost like a spice, dried and added to desserts. Because several small brown mushrooms grow in the same debris, experienced foragers check each individual rather than a whole patch.

## Memorable fact

The maple scent comes from sotolon, the same compound that gives fenugreek and maple syrup their smell.
