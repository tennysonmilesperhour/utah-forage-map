## Identification

The cap is pale salmon pink with darker concentric zones, and its margin is rolled in and covered with long woolly hairs. Cut gills release white latex that tastes very hot. It grows with birch.

## Why it matters

The woolly milk cap causes gastrointestinal poisoning when eaten without extensive processing. It is eaten in parts of northern Europe after parboiling and salting, which is not a safe home experiment.

## Memorable fact

Its species name torminosus means causing colic.
