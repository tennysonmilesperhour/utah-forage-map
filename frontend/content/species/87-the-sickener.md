## Identification

The cap is bright scarlet, and its skin peels almost to the center, revealing pink flesh. Gills and stem are pure white. The flesh is brittle and crumbly. A tiny piece tasted and spat out burns intensely.

## Why it matters

The sickener is a classic example of a gastrointestinal irritant mushroom. It forms partnerships with conifers in damp woods.

## Memorable fact

Its scientific name emetica refers to vomiting, the effect it causes.
