## Identification

The cap is olive brown to dark brown, dotted with small, pure white warts, and its margin is finely grooved. Gills and stem are white. The ring is smooth. The stem base sits in a rounded bulb with a distinct rim, like a collar, left by the volva.

## Why it matters

Panther cap poisoning resembles fly agaric poisoning but is often more severe, with drowsiness, delirium, and occasionally seizures within a few hours. It grows across Europe, western Asia, and North Africa; North American look-alikes belong to related species.

## Memorable fact

The pure white warts on a brown cap inspired the name, comparing it to a spotted big cat.
