## Identification

The caps are fan- or funnel-shaped and creamy white, often with brown, orange, or purple tints near the center. Gills run down the short stem. Clusters grow on wood. In darkness, the gills glow green.

## Why it matters

Ghost fungus contains illudin S, which causes severe vomiting within an hour or two of eating. People have been poisoned after confusing it with edible oyster mushrooms.

## Memorable fact

Its glow is bright enough to see with dark-adapted eyes, and night walks are organized to find it.
