## Identification

Golden oysters grow in dense, bouquet-like clusters of bright lemon-yellow caps. The gills are white and run down short, often off-center stems. The flesh is thin and delicate.

## Uses and ecology

Golden oysters are cultivated and were first reported wild in North America in the 2010s. Research suggests they can displace native wood-decay fungi, so eating wild ones and not spreading spawn outdoors both help.

## Memorable fact

It has spread across much of eastern North America within about two decades of escaping cultivation.
