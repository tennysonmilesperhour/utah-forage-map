## Identification

The cap is smooth, brown, sometimes with darker radial streaks. The gills are free from the stem, white when young, then pink. The stem has no ring or volva. It grows on dead wood, sometimes buried.

## Uses and ecology

Deer mushrooms are common wood decomposers across the Northern Hemisphere. They are edible but often considered bland.

## Memorable fact

It can fruit in every month without hard frost, making it one of the longest-season woodland mushrooms.
