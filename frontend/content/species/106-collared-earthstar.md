## Identification

The outer layer splits into four to eight pointed rays that fold back like a star. The central spore sac sits in a saucer-like collar formed by the inner layer. A pore at the top releases puffs of brown spores when raindrops hit.

## Uses and ecology

Earthstars decompose leaf litter. The collared earthstar is one of the largest and most common.

## Memorable fact

Raindrops act as bellows, puffing spores from the sac.
