## Identification

The cup is bright scarlet inside and pale, whitish, and downy outside, often on a short stem. It grows on dead hardwood sticks lying in moss or leaf litter. It appears in the cold months.

## Uses and ecology

Scarlet elf cups decompose fallen sticks and are indicators of clean air and damp woodland. Their vivid color in winter makes them a favorite for photographers.

## Memorable fact

It is one of the few mushrooms that fruits in the depth of winter, sometimes showing red against snow.
