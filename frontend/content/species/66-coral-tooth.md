## Identification

The fruiting body is a white, delicate, branching structure. Short spines hang in rows along the branches like a comb. It grows from dead hardwood logs and stumps. It yellows with age.

## Uses and ecology

Coral tooth decays dead hardwood and is protected in some countries where old forests are scarce. It has a delicate, seafood-like texture when cooked.

## Memorable fact

Its many fine branches make it look like frozen coral or icicles.
