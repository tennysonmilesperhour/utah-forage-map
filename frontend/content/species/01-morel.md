## Identification

A true morel has a honeycomb surface made of pits and ridges, not loose wrinkles. Slice the entire mushroom from cap to base: the inside should form **one clean hollow chamber**. The cap joins the stem along its lower edge, and the mushroom grows from soil rather than wood.

North American yellow morels are often treated as *Morchella americana* or *M. esculentoides* rather than the European *M. esculenta*. Local taxonomy varies, but the field checks above remain essential.

## Uses and ecology

Morels are widely prized for a nutty flavor and dry well, but they must be thoroughly cooked. A 2023 Montana restaurant outbreak linked raw or undercooked morels with 51 illnesses and two deaths. Western burn morels can fruit heavily during the first spring after wildfire; elsewhere, look around dying elms, ash, old orchards, and cottonwood bottoms.

## Memorable fact

Commercial crews follow the previous summer's wildfires across the West each spring, searching for brief mass flushes.
