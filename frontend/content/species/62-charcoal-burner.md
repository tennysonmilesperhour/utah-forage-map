## Identification

The cap mixes violet, lilac, gray, and green, often in patches. Unlike most Russulas, its white gills are flexible and greasy and do not shatter when rubbed. The stem is white. The taste is mild.

## Uses and ecology

The charcoal burner is one of the most common edible Russulas in European woods. It is mycorrhizal with broadleaf trees.

## Memorable fact

Its flexible gills are unusual for a genus famous for brittle, crumbly gills.
