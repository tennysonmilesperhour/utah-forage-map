## Identification

The cap is pale hazel to light brown, dry, and often cracks in dry weather. The stem is club-shaped and covered from top to bottom in a fine, pale net. Pores are white, maturing yellowish olive, and do not bruise blue. It appears earlier in the year than most porcini.

## Uses and ecology

Summer boletes partner with oaks and beeches. They are prized in Europe and are often infested by insect larvae in warm weather, so check each cap.

## Memorable fact

It is often the first porcini of the year, sometimes appearing in late spring.
