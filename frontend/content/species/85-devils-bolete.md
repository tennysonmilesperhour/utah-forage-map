## Identification

The cap is pale whitish to gray, large and stout. Pores are bright red, and the swollen stem is covered in a red net. Cut flesh turns pale blue. Older specimens smell unpleasant.

## Why it matters

Devil's bolete causes gastrointestinal poisoning. It is uncommon and protected in some countries, but its red pores are the key lesson for bolete hunters.

## Memorable fact

Its old reputation led to the name satanas, although it is not considered deadly.
