## Identification

Caps are white to cream, thick-fleshed, and often irregular. The gills are very crowded and white. The stout stem has no ring or volva. The smell is strongly mealy, like fresh flour or cucumber. It fruits in spring, often in rings in grass.

## Uses and ecology

St George's mushroom is a traditional spring edible in Europe, named for its appearance near St George's Day on 23 April. Its spring timing overlaps with dangerous white mushrooms, so every specimen needs checking.

## Memorable fact

Rings of this mushroom can persist in the same pasture for decades.
