## Identification

Caps are glossy, sticky, and orange to tawny brown, darker in the center. The gills are pale yellow. The stem is the key feature: pale near the cap and dark brown and velvety toward the base. It grows in tight clusters on wood in cold weather and has no ring.

## Uses and ecology

Velvet shank decays dead hardwood and tolerates freezing. The white, long-stemmed enoki sold in stores is grown from related strains in dark, carbon-dioxide-rich conditions, which is why it looks so different.

## Memorable fact

Fruiting bodies can freeze solid and thaw to keep releasing spores.
