## Identification

Caps are small, flat to slightly depressed, and whitish with a frosty or chalky surface. Gills are crowded and run down the stem. It grows in grass, sometimes in rings, next to fairy ring mushrooms.

## Why it matters

Muscarine poisoning begins within 15 minutes to two hours. Symptoms include heavy sweating, salivation, tears, blurred vision, and a slow heart rate. It is treatable, but serious in children and people with heart disease.

## Memorable fact

Its old name, sweating mushroom, describes the main symptom it causes.
