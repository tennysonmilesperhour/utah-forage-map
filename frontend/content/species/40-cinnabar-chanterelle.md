## Identification

Cinnabar chanterelles are small, rarely wider than a few centimeters, and vivid cinnabar red to pink. The underside has shallow, blunt, forked ridges in a similar color that run down the stem. Flesh is firm and white inside. They often fruit in large numbers along trails after summer rain.

## Uses and ecology

These mycorrhizal mushrooms associate mainly with oaks. Their color survives cooking better than most, but their size means collecting many for a meal. Take a modest share from each patch.

## Memorable fact

The red color comes from canthaxanthin, a carotenoid also used as a food coloring.
