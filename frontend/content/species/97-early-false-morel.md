## Identification

The cap is wrinkled and brown and attached only at the very top of the stem, so it hangs like a thimble over a finger. Slice it lengthwise: the stem is filled with cottony pith. It fruits early in spring near cottonwoods and aspens.

## Why it matters

Older guides list it as edible with caution, but it has caused poisonings, and it teaches a key morel check: slice every specimen top to bottom.

## Memorable fact

Its spores are among the largest of any mushroom, visible as distinct grains under a basic microscope.
