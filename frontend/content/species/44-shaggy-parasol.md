## Identification

The cap is covered with coarse, shaggy, upturned scales over a white background, with a smooth brown center. The stem is smooth, without a snakeskin pattern, and ends in a swollen bulb. Cut the flesh and it turns saffron orange, then reddish brown. The ring is thick and movable.

## Uses and ecology

Shaggy parasols often fruit in rich, disturbed soil and compost. Many people eat them without trouble, but a significant minority react with gastrointestinal symptoms. It is not a beginner mushroom.

## Memorable fact

The red-staining flesh separates this group from the true parasol within seconds of cutting.
