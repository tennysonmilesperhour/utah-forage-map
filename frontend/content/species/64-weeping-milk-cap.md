## Identification

The cap is orange-brown to tawny and velvety. Cut the gills and white latex pours out, staining brown as it dries. The flesh smells fishy, especially as it ages. The stem is colored like the cap.

## Uses and ecology

This milk cap partners with hardwoods and conifers in Europe, Asia, and North America. It is widely eaten and fries well.

## Memorable fact

A single cut can produce enough latex to drip, which is how it earned the name weeping.
